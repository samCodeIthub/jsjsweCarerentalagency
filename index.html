Why I built weCare

Hello, I'm Sam. I completed my BSc in Computer Science at the University of Cape Coast (UCC), and I built weCare myself.

Finding a safe, honest place to stay is one of the hardest parts of life as a college student, and families and working people face the same struggle looking for a good apartment. weCare exists to take the stress out of that search, so you can see what is available, what it costs, and book with confidence.

We are here for you.
